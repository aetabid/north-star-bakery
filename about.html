About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery started as a small family kitchen with one oven and a big love for bread. Neighbors kept asking for our loaves, so we opened a shop on Main Street. Today we still bake everything by hand, and we still know many of our customers by name.

Where Our Ingredients Come From

We buy flour from regional mills, eggs and dairy from nearby farms, and fruit that is in season. Using local ingredients keeps our baking fresh and supports the community that supports us.
